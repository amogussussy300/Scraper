AUCTION SALE WEDNESDAY 1ST APRIL 2026


For sale by public auction at The Brooks Suite, Silverlake Stadium, Stoneham Lane, Eastleigh, Southampton, SO50 9HT.
Located just a short walk from Southampton General Hospital, this second-floor flat offers excellent potential for first-time buyers and investors alike.
The property boasts a generous layout throughout, including a large double bedroom and a bright, open-plan living/dining room with ample space for both a comfortable seating area and dining table. The kitchen is particularly spacious, providing extensive cupboard and worktop space, along with room for multiple appliances. Completing the accommodation is a bathroom fitted with a bathtub, hand basin and WC.
Externally, the flat benefits from the rare advantage of a private garage, offering additional storage or secure parking.
Further benefits include no forward chain, a long lease with the remainder of 999 years, and low service charges, making this an attractive and practical purchase.
From an investment perspective, the flat’s close proximity to Southampton General Hospital makes it particularly appealing. With the hospital being one of the city’s largest employers, there is a consistent demand for rental accommodation from healthcare professionals, ranging from doctors and nurses to support staff. This strong and reliable tenant pool, combined with the property’s low service charges and long lease, ensures excellent rental potential and long-term investment security.

Location
Coxford Road enjoys a prime position, just a short walk from Southampton General Hospital, local schools, and convenient bus routes. Everyday amenities are within easy reach, including Tesco Express, M&S Food Hall, Sainsburys Supermarket and Shirley High Street. Larger green spaces such as Southampton Common and the Sports Centre. With excellent access to Southampton City Centre, mainline train stations, and the motorway network, this location offers superb connectivity for commuters and families alike.
